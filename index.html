Overview

Rally Flag pulls together everything we keep looking up while playing and streaming Destiny 2, so it lives in one place instead of a dozen scattered tabs.

About Us

We're the people behind Rally Flag — not a company, just a few of us who got tired of digging through a dozen sites every raid night, so we built the tool we actually wanted.

Everything here comes from our own playtime and our own runs: the rolls we chase, the builds we actually use, the puzzle solutions we've verified ourselves. When we're not sure about a mechanic, we don't guess. We test it, or we leave it out until we can.

Raid smarter, not harder is more than a tagline to us. No gatekeeping, no "read the guide or get left behind" — whether it's your first raid ever or your five-hundredth, we'd rather teach you something than watch you struggle.

Not Done With Destiny

On September 21, 2026, Bungie told the community something a lot of people had stopped expecting to hear: they're not done with Destiny. New leadership announced they're committing to unvaulting the campaigns, destinations, and raids that got pulled after Beyond Light — content they called "what's rightfully yours" — alongside new stories still to come. It won't be fast; restoring that content means real engine work, not a switch to flip.

None of this happens without the people who kept showing up anyway. Every raid night, every god roll chased, every guide someone bothered to write for a friend — that's what told Bungie this game was still worth building for. We started Rally Flag for the same reason, and we've got no plans to stop either. Help us keep up with whatever's coming back and whatever's new:

Site FAQ
Is this official?
No. Rally Flag is an unofficial fan project. We're not affiliated with or endorsed by Bungie.
Where does the data come from?
A mix of our own in-game verification and trusted community sources like light.gg and GameRant, plus Bungie's own public API for anything live: god rolls, Xur, and weekly milestones pull real-time data straight from Bungie.
How current is it?
We update it as the game changes: new seasons, new artifacts, balance passes. If something's out of date, it means we haven't caught up to it yet, not that it's been abandoned.
Something looks wrong? What do I do?
Let us know. We'd rather fix a mistake than leave it up, and we don't publish anything we haven't been able to verify.
Is it free?
Yes. No accounts, no paywalls, no ads.
Does it work on mobile?
Yes. The whole site is built to work on phones and tablets, not just desktop.
