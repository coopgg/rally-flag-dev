Rally Flag

Privacy Policy

For the Rally Flag website and the RallyBot Discord bot.

Last updated: October 2026

RallyBot (Discord)

RallyBot posts Destiny 2 rotation info (the Distortion tracker and featured weekly raid/dungeon) to Discord, either when you run a slash command or on a fixed schedule. It's intentionally minimal:

  • No message content access. RallyBot doesn't request Discord's message content intent — it can't read the messages you send.
  • No stored user data. When you run /distortion or /thisweek, Discord's own API tells the bot who ran it and where, same as any bot — but RallyBot doesn't log, store, or reuse that. It computes an answer and replies. Nothing about you is written anywhere.
  • The only thing it stores is which rotation hour/week it last posted about, so it doesn't double-post after a restart — no personal or server data, just two numbers.
  • No third-party sharing. RallyBot doesn't send any data anywhere except Discord's own API, and it doesn't call Bungie's API either — its rotation data is computed locally from a fixed pattern.

To remove RallyBot's access entirely, remove it from your server via Server Settings → Integrations at any time.

rallyflag.gg (the website)

  • The site itself is static pages plus client-side JavaScript. The one exception is the Locker preview, which uses two small Cloudflare services described in its own section below.
  • Pages that show Destiny data (raids, dungeons, builds, etc.) call Bungie's public API directly from your browser using a shared, non-personal API key. No account or personal info is required to use them.
  • The optional "sign in with Bungie.net" feature on Vendors uses Bungie's own OAuth login to show your loadouts. The resulting access token is kept in your browser's sessionStorage only — it's never sent to us, because there's no "us" to send it to. It's cleared automatically when you close the tab. (The Locker is different; see below.)
  • We use Cloudflare Web Analytics to see aggregate traffic (page views, referrers, rough location by country). It's cookieless and doesn't fingerprint or track you individually — Cloudflare's own privacy policy covers exactly what it collects. We don't run any other analytics, tracking pixels, or advertising on the site.

Locker (preview)

The Locker shows your weapons and armor after you sign in with Bungie.net, and lets you move, equip and save loadouts. Here's what it keeps and where.

  • In your browser: your sign-in token, farming mode and a short note of your recent moves (sessionStorage, cleared when the tab closes); your tags (Favorite, Junk and the rest), PvE/PvP marks, item notes, weapon wish lists, saved loadouts and layout choices (localStorage); and a trimmed copy of Bungie's public item database (IndexedDB) so the page loads faster next time.
  • Loadout and tag sync: when you're signed in, your saved loadouts, tags, PvE/PvP marks, item notes and wish lists are also stored on Rally Flag's sync service (a Cloudflare Worker with a Cloudflare D1 database) so they survive clearing your browser and show up on your other devices. What's stored is your Destiny account id and, per loadout: its name, the ids and types of its items, and its subclass, artifact perk, mod, shader and ornament choices. Also stored: when it was last saved, and for 60 days, the ids of loadouts you deleted, so your other devices delete them too. Per tagged or noted item: its id, its tag, its PvE/PvP mark, its note, and when you last changed them (a removed tag or mark is kept the same way for 60 days). Per wish-listed weapon: its name, the perk names you picked, and when you last changed them. No name, email or other personal details.
  • Your sign-in token and the sync service: each sync request includes your Bungie sign-in token so the service can ask Bungie which account it belongs to. That's how it makes sure only you can read or change your loadouts. It uses the token for that check only. It doesn't store or log the token; it remembers the answer for up to 10 minutes under a one-way hash of the token.
  • Importing a DIM link: when you import a dim.gg link, the link goes through a small Rally Flag Cloudflare Worker that fetches that public DIM page and returns its loadout (dim.gg doesn't allow browsers to read it directly). No sign-in or account information is sent with it. The answer may be cached for 10 minutes.
  • Deleting your synced data: deleting a loadout or removing a tag in the Locker removes it from the sync service too; the service keeps only its id for 60 days so your other devices remove it as well. Delete my saved loadouts and tags (at the bottom of the Loadouts tab, while signed in) removes your account's entry from the sync service entirely, ids included, and your other devices clear their copies the next time they sync.
  • Share links: pressing Share on a saved loadout (signed in) stores a copy of that one loadout on the sync service under a short random id, and gives you a link with that id. The copy holds the loadout's name, class, the types of its items (not your item ids), and its subclass, artifact perk and mod choices (not its shaders or ornaments), plus when it was made. Anyone with the link can see it and import it into their own Locker; opening one needs no sign-in, and it doesn't show your name or account. To let you delete your links later, each copy also stores a one-way hash of your Destiny account id; that's never shown to anyone who opens the link. Delete my share links (bottom of the Loadouts tab) removes every link you've made: they stop working for everyone, but loadouts people already imported from them stay in their Locker. Share links aren't removed by Delete my saved loadouts and tags.
  • The Locker only moves, equips or changes your gear when you press a button, and every change goes straight from your browser to Bungie.
  • Community rolls (off unless you switch them on under Tools): the Locker downloads DIM's community wish list (Voltron) from GitHub (raw.githubusercontent.com) at most once a week and keeps the recommended rolls for weapons you own in your browser (IndexedDB). Nothing about you or your gear is sent; it's a plain file download.
  • Perk insights from Clarity (when offered, on unless you switch them off under Tools): the first time you tap a perk, the Locker downloads Clarity's community perk descriptions from GitHub (raw.githubusercontent.com) and keeps them in your browser (IndexedDB). Each visit it checks a small version file there and downloads them again only when Clarity has published an update. Nothing about you or your gear is sent.
  • New items: to mark items that are new since your last visit, the Locker keeps the ids of the items it has shown you in your browser (localStorage).

Your Week (preview)

Your Week shows your progress against this week's rotation after you sign in with Bungie.net. It reads your season pass, milestones, bounties, quests, seals and each character's raid and dungeon activity history from Bungie when you open the page. None of that is stored or sent anywhere else; only a trimmed copy of Bungie's public definitions (names and objective texts) is kept in your browser (IndexedDB) so the page loads faster next time.
