Heroic Public Events

Every public event has a hidden trigger that flips it Heroic: tougher, but noticeably better loot.

Public events always give better rewards on Heroic — more Legendary engrams, more XP, and a shot at some event-exclusive drops. It's worth doing on any destination, but especially one that's currently Distorted, since that already bumps the gear tier on everything else you pick up out there. The exact trigger below can shift a little between seasons, but the core mechanic for each event has stayed the same.

Cosmodrome
Devil Walker
Take down all three shield generators around the Walker before it can recover any of them. A second Walker spawns in — kill both of them to finish the Heroic event.
Cabal locations
Injection Rig
Each Elite Psion you kill opens a set of three vents on the Rig for that tier. Shoot just one of the three to advance — do that for all three tiers to trigger Heroic and spawn the boss.
EDZ, Nessus
Glimmer Extraction
A single Glimmer Refinery node spawns during each of the event's three phases. Destroy it every phase (three total), then stand in the resulting glimmer pool to capture it while the Fallen keep coming.
Cabal locations
Cabal Excavation
While defending the drill, a Cabal Thresher dropship periodically flies in — shoot it down before it leaves the area. Doing so spawns a yellow-bar Centurion for the Heroic finish.
Fallen locations
Cryo-Pod Escape
Damage the escapee that pops out of the cryo-pod until the pod starts steaming, then finish it with a Stasis freeze instead of a kill to trigger Heroic.
Fallen locations
Ether Resupply
The main Servitor is shielded by several smaller support Servitors. Kill every one of them, not just enough to drop the shield, before finishing the main target.
Hive locations (Moon, Throne World)
Witches' Ritual
Stand on one summoning plate to expose the Witch on that side and kill her, then repeat on the other plate for the second Witch. With both down, two Hive crystals appear over the portal — shoot both quickly while still on the plates to trigger Heroic and pull a boss Ogre out of the portal.
Nessus
Conflux
Follow the lines radiating out from the central conflux to the three capture zones and hold all three at once. Filling the meter before it resets triggers Heroic and spawns a large flying Hydra to finish it off.
Europa
Crux Convergence
Each of the two Darkness Crux points has three scanner drones orbiting above it. Destroy all three at both points before killing the Fallen Brig — the event renames itself "Fallen Brigs" once you've triggered Heroic.
