Destiny 2 · Tools

Tools

The outside tools we use and recommend, and where Rally Flag has its own.

None of these are part of Rally Flag; they open in a new tab.

Gear and weapons

  • Destiny Item Manager (opens in a new tab)

    app.destinyitemmanager.com

    Move gear between characters and the vault, tag it and build loadouts.

    On Rally Flag: Locker →

  • D2 Armor Picker (opens in a new tab)

    d2armorpicker.com

    Finds the best armor stat spread from what you own.

    On Rally Flag: Locker armor optimizer →

  • light.gg (opens in a new tab)

    light.gg

    Every weapon's possible rolls, and what most of the community runs.

    On Rally Flag: Weapon Lookup →

  • Godroll.tv (opens in a new tab)

    godroll.tv

    Weapon rolls with their hidden stats and community picks.

    On Rally Flag: Weapon Lookup →

  • D2 Arsenal (opens in a new tab)

    d2-arsenal.vercel.app

    Every roll a weapon can get, searchable by perk, origin trait, element and ammo.

    On Rally Flag: Weapon Lookup →

  • D2 Foundry (opens in a new tab)

    d2foundry.gg

    The original weapon roll and stat tool.

Builds and perks

  • Mobalytics Destiny 2 (opens in a new tab)

    mobalytics.gg

    Build guides for every class and subclass.

    On Rally Flag: Builds →

  • Clarity (opens in a new tab)

    d2clarity.com

    Community research on what perks and abilities really do, with the numbers.

    On Rally Flag: Weapon Lookup →

  • Destiny Data Compendium (opens in a new tab)

    Google Sheets

    A community spreadsheet of exact numbers: damage buffs and debuffs, how they stack, and what perks and abilities do.
